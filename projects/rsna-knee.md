# RSNA Knee Abnormality Detection

## My Contributions

**Primary contributor to model development and experimentation**

Took primary responsibility for much of the model development and experimentation, adapting deep learning approaches for multi-label knee MRI analysis.

- Developed and adapted model experiments using CoAtNet, EfficientNet-B0, ConvNeXt-Tiny, and ResNet34-based approaches.
- Worked on attention-based aggregation and gated multiple-instance learning for combining information across MRI slices.
- Explored DINOv2-based self-supervised learning and pseudo-labeling workflows, alongside ensemble and inference pipelines.
- Built on existing architectures, pretrained models, and public baselines as part of a collaborative competition project.
